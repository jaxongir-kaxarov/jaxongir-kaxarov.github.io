### **1C Programming Course (21 days) - Homework explanation**

#### **Course structure:**

- **21 homework assignments** — one after each day
- After each homework there is a **teacher's solution** (video)
- Use the video solution for **self-assessment**

#### **Self-assessment rules:**

- If you find errors — fix them in your own database
- **100% identical match is not required**
- Example: "Sotuv" vs "Realizatsiya" — name differences are not important
- **Main point:** the business logic must work correctly
- All conditions must be satisfied

#### **Homework 1:**

- **The easiest task** in the course
- Fully based on theoretical material
- Repeating every step from the theory is not mandatory
