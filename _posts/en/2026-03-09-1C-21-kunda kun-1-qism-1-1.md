# 1C 8.3 programming in 21 days COURSE

## Introductory video synopsis

---

### 1. COURSE AUTHOR AND GENERAL INFORMATION

**Author:** Evgeniy Gilyov

**Course name:** "Learn Programming in 1C in 21 Days"

**Video type:** Introductory video

---

### 2. COURSE GOALS

| Main goal | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Introduction | Introduce the learner to the 1C:Enterprise 8 platform |
| Practice | View and create almost all platform objects |
| Skills | Learn platform capabilities, solve simple tasks, acquire basic programming skills |

---

### 3. PRACTICAL PART (END-TO-END EXAMPLE)

**Example:** Automation system for a furniture company

**Areas to automate:**

- ✅ Sales operations
- ✅ Accounting
- ✅ Payroll calculation

**Approach:** Broad objects are used, but the course does not go into details

**Main principle:** For each task, the simplest and clearest solution is chosen (clarity over optimality)

**Future plan:** Optimization and details will be covered in advanced follow-up courses

---
