## Overview of the 1C:Enterprise System

### 1. System users

The 1C:Enterprise system is widely used in the following roles:

- **Accountants** – data entry, preparing tax and fund reports
- **Managers** – profit, growth dynamics, plan-vs-actual analysis
- **All staff** – warehouse workers, sales managers, logisticians, cashiers, and others

> The system is primarily designed to automate business processes.

---

### 2. The system's three main components

| Component | Definition | Analogy (Word/Excel example) |
|-----------|------------|------------------------------|
| **Platform** | Developer tools and the runtime environment | Computer (technical base) |
| **Configuration** | Settings that define the system's functionality | The Word or Excel application itself |
| **Database** | Where user-entered data is stored | Created documents (files) |

**Example:** Word and Excel are configurations, the documents created in them are the database, and the computer is the platform.

---

### 3. Types of configurations

- **Typical solutions** – common, ready-made configurations:
  - 1C:Accounting 8
  - 1C:Trade Management
  - 1C:Payroll and HR Management
  - 1C:Production Management (being replaced by 1C:ERP)

- **Custom (tailored) solutions** – adapted to a specific company's needs

---

### 4. Platform capabilities

- Create custom configurations
- Built-in high-level language (designed for business tasks)
- Rapid development to automate business processes

---

### 5. System implementation

When a company adopts 1C, the following stages are usually carried out:

1. Install the platform and the database
2. Train users
3. Describe business processes (who enters what data and where)
4. Adapt a typical solution to the company's needs (if necessary)

**Specialists:**

- **1C developer** – technical setup
- **1C consultant** – business process analysis

---

### Main conclusion

1C:Enterprise is a comprehensive business automation system made of platform + configuration + database. It can be quickly deployed using typical solutions and customized for specific needs.
